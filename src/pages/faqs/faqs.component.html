Booking & Payment

Q: How do I make a booking?
A: Use the booking form on the site or contact our support. You will receive a confirmation once availability and payment are processed.
Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept major debit/credit cards, UPI and bank transfers. Details are shown during checkout.

Cancellations & Changes

Q: Can I change my booking dates?
A: Changes depend on supplier rules. Contact us as soon as possible; additional fees may apply.
Q: How do I cancel a booking?
A: Contact support with your booking reference. Cancellation fees vary by product and supplier.

Refunds

Q: When will I receive a refund?
A: If approved, refunds are processed to the original payment method. Timing depends on the payment provider and supplier.

Travel Documents

Q: What documents do I need?
A: Valid ID, passport and visas (where required). Check destination rules before travel.
